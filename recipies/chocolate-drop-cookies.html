Chocolate Drop Cookies

Description

This recipie is one my Grandmother made for me growing up. It was actually the first recipe she tought me how to make. I make it every christmas and they last maybe a couple of hours anywhere I take them.

Cooking Equpiment

  • Oven
  • Stand Mixer (not required but very helpful)
  • Measuring Cups
  • Cookie Sheet (any size will work but the bigger you have the less batches you will have to do)

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Cup Butter
  • 1/2 Cup Peanut Butter (I prefer crunchy but both creamy and crunchy works)
  • 1/2 Cup Sugar
  • 1/2 Cup Brown Sugar
  • 1 unbeaten egg
  • 1 tsp. Vanilla
  • 1/2 Cup Flour
  • Unwrapped Hershey Chocolate Kisses
  • Extra sugar for rolling dough balls into

Cooking Directions

  1. Pre-heat oven to 375
  2. Cream together butter and peanut butter
  3. Gradually add in other ingredients, order does not matter, but I usually do flour last.
  4. Once dough is full mixed roll into half inch balls
  5. Roll dough balls in sugar
  6. Line on a lightly greased baking pan 1 inch apart
  7. Bake at 375 for 8 minutes (these cookies can burn very easily so watch them)
  8. Remove from the over and press Hershey kisses in the top.
